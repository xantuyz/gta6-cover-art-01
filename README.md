Vice Cover Studio turns your own photos into a bold multi-panel poster cover — sunset grades, original procedural art (striped sun, palms, skyline, halftone), thick poster lettering — and exports a share-ready PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350, or 1920×1080. Fully offline: your photos never leave your browser.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — pick up to 3 photos, choose a layout and grade, type a title, and hit Export PNG.

## What it does

- **3 multi-panel layouts** — Vice Grid (hero + two panels), Sunset Strip (wide hero + three below), Neon Trio (three tall panels) — each mixing your photos with original drawn art.
- **3 colour grades** — Sunset Vice City, Neon Night, Palm Dusk — plus a vignette and halftone finish.
- **Any photo** — upload up to 3 photos or drag-drop them in; one photo across all panels or one per panel; drag a panel to reposition, scroll to zoom, rotate, fit, fill.
- **Poster lettering** — auto-fitting bold title (Anton, SIL OFL, vendored under `app/fonts/`) with outline and shadow, plus a sub-caption, position and colour options.
- **Fast export** — one click renders the cover at full resolution and downloads the PNG.

All artwork is drawn procedurally in code; no logos, artwork, or fonts from any game or brand are used.
